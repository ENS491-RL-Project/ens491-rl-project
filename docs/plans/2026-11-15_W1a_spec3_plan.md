## Why

- **W1a:** spec 2, scored as it acts (sampled), passed on 6 of 10 training seeds; the rule needs 7.
  All ten pass from their own start states; the four that fail, fail at the handoff (0.874–0.892).
- **Diagnostic:** with the dropped key removed from the grid, the four failing seeds go from 0.880
  to 0.985 pooled, and all ten are at 0.967 or above. Doubling the time gives 0.921. In every
  failed episode the box comes into view at some point (within the first 3 steps in 74%, by step 17
  at the latest). Reading: **Key**, a property of the skill, not of the failing seeds.
- **Key-position measurement (491 valid handoff states, env seeds 51000–51499):** the key is never
  orthogonally adjacent to the door (0/491), so `key_off_door`'s docstring is right and revision
  1's "cause on record" was wrong. What the handoff does look like, in **491 of 491** states: the
  agent stands on the cell in front of the door, facing along the wall (264 south, 227 north, never
  the door), with the key on the cell directly in front of it. Spec 2's random-pose hook produces
  this configuration only rarely; how rarely was not measured.
- So the mismatch is the **start state**: one narrow, always-the-same situation the skill was hardly
  trained in. The counterfactual shows that the key's presence there causes the failures. It does
  not show that training in that situation fixes them. This plan tests that.

## The one design (no arms)

Spec 3 = spec 2 with **half of the training episodes started at the real handoff**:

- with probability 0.5: spec 2's hook (`open_door`, `key_off_door`, random pose). Same
  distribution as spec 2, not the same layouts: the coin flip consumes a draw from the env's RNG;
- with probability 0.5: UnlockPickup from the env's own reset, then the frozen `unlock_door` and
  `drop_held` run to their ends (argmax, as they are frozen today); `pickup_box` starts in the
  state they leave. If the chain does not leave the handoff state (door open, hand empty), the
  layout is rejected and redrawn (`UnsuitableLayout`).

Everything else is spec 2: goal, initiation, timeout 120, env, PPO config, budget 1,000,000,
`eval_every` 20,000. The hook gets its own spec string, so spec 3 has its own `spec_hash`.

**This is a new mechanism, not a precedent.** `open_door_tk` built its parent's distribution with a
hand-written hook. Here frozen nodes are *executed inside a reset*. Consequences to handle:
`SubgoalEnv` hands the hook the raw env, so the hook must build the 147-dim normalised observation
the frozen nodes expect; the chain uses env steps (mean 16.8, at most 32 in the measurement), which
leaves the 120-step timeout well inside the env's 288; it relies on `DummyVecEnv` (one process).

Why a mixture: the old half keeps spec 2's distribution, so a change in the result is attributable
to the added half and the own-hook row of the test stays meaningful.

## Criterion (W1a's, unchanged)

- A row passes at sampled success ≥ 0.90 over valid episodes with at least 475 of 500 valid; fewer
  valid → "not determined (chain)" for that row.
- A training seed passes if both rows pass.
- Seeds 43–47: 4 or 5 pass → **passes**; 0–2 → **fails**; exactly 3 → train 48–52 the same way and
  require at least 7 of 10.
