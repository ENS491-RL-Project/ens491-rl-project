## 6. Deviations from the amendment and the plan

1. **The two library functions landed in H-7a.0, not H-7a.1.** `h7_nodes.py` cannot import the
   amendment's specs without `carrying_anything()` and `key_off_door()`, and the suite had to stay
   green at every commit. Their tests and the audit are H-7a.1.
2. **`key_off_door` rejects 27/500 first draws, not 13.** The amendment's 13 was attempt 1's
   flood-fill count: the key on the approach cell *and* blocking the only path, with half the hands
   full. That is a different quantity, and the measurement wins.
3. **The attempt-1 checkpoint moved to `runs/v2/attempts/0-4_pickup_box_attempt1/`**, beside the
   renamed reports and logs. The registry keys by node id, so the new `pickup_box` would otherwise
   have overwritten it. `pickup_box_attempt1_spec()` keeps its hash (`eedc2100c1b0e30e`) loadable.
   The new spec's hash is `a529aa4e74808ba0`.
4. **`train_unlock_door.py` got `--no-freeze` and `--n-steps`.** The plan's re-measurement command
   assumed both. Without `--no-freeze`, a stable re-measurement would have overwritten the frozen
   node every later node depends on. The budget (51,200 decisions, as H-6.8) and `--skip-ablation`
   are choices the plan left open.
5. **`eval_h7_preflight.py --stage pickup_box --unstable-pickup-box`.** After A2b failed, the A2a
   row is still taken as a gate. The `pickup_box` rows come from the TRAINING checkpoint, are labelled
   diagnostic, and never gate. `gate_passed` is **false**, with `gate_failed_reason` naming A2b; a node
   that never froze must fail the gate even though no gating row fell below 0.90.
6. **Regression 9 of 9, not 10 of 10.** The amendment's count assumed `pickup_box` frozen.
7. **The §8 arm stage records two more things than H-6.9 wrote it to:** the held-out deterministic
   first-key accuracy by door colour with Wilson bounds, which §8 asks for, and "collapsed vs
   tilted" from the final probe. The rule (≥ 0.9 on one key for both colours) is the amendment's and
   was coded before any result was seen.
8. **`diagnose_pickup_box.py --spec2`** is a post hoc diagnostic, not in the amendment. It writes its
   own file and leaves attempt 1's diagnosis unchanged.
9. **Launched on battery.** On the author's explicit permission for this run, against the
   handoff's "mains power" note. It affects wall time only.
10. **`train_blocked_pickup.py` and `--stage blocked` refuse to run** until H-7b is planned. T7's
    children changed, so their builders no longer match, and nothing BlockedUnlockPickup was to be
    trained.

### 12.1 E.0: what `STOCHASTIC` samples

In `src/skills/executor.py` the mode reaches the policies in exactly two places:
`top.policy.act(obs, deterministic=self.deterministic)` for a primitive on top of the stack, and
`top.policy.select(obs, mask, deterministic=self.deterministic)` for a composite. `deterministic`
is `execution_mode == "deterministic"`, and nothing overrides it per node or per level. So under
`STOCHASTIC` **both levels sample**: every composite selector on the stack and every primitive.

Run to confirm:

- `tests/test_executor.py::test_execution_mode_is_passed_to_policies` passes. It covers one composite
  over one primitive and asserts only the primitive's flag.
- In the audit script, every policy of `{2,1} solve_doorkey`'s graph (`solve_doorkey`,
  `unlock_door`, `get_key`, `open_door`, `go_to_goal`) was wrapped with a recorder, and episodes ran
  until all five had been called: 1 episode in deterministic mode, 3 in stochastic mode. Every call
  received `deterministic=True` in deterministic mode. **Every call, at both levels, received
  `deterministic=False` in stochastic mode.**

Two consequences of the code, stated as read:

- A node used only to build a start state (the `prepare` chains below) runs through its own
  `Executor()`, which defaults to deterministic.
- Any mode string other than `"deterministic"` samples. v2 §5.3 also names an `epsilon` mode, but
  the Executor defines no epsilon behaviour.

   labelled as deterministic.
3. **E.1 harness and seed split.** Each `env_id` gets its own 50 seeds; the regression splits 50
   seeds across a node's envs. Primitives are scored on `SubgoalEnv` under their own hook, as the
