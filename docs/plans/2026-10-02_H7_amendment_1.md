## 1. The decision

H-7 stopped on A2: `{0,4} pickup_box` was not stable within 600,000 env steps on any seed
(held-out 0.28 / 0.40 / 0.24). Of the three options in the stop report, the choice is **a different
node specification**, in the form the architecture itself prescribes: **split the skill.**

Why not the other two:

- **More budget on the same spec** buys the wrong thing. The seed-42 checkpoint scores 0.28 under
  its own hook and 0.10 at the real handoff (`runs/v2/pickup_box_diagnosis.json`): after 600k steps
  it had learned the starts beside the box, which no parent ever produces.
- **Reporting it as is** ends H-7 with no reuse measurement at all, while flat PPO's side is already
  in: 0/50 at all 30 evaluations on UnlockPickup, three seeds, 3,000,320 steps each.

Why splitting: the node as specified in the plan asked one flat policy for three things under one
sparse reward: cross the door, drop the key, pick up the box, on two environments and with the hand
randomised. That was the advisor's specification error, not a property of the task. The diagnosis
shows the first of the three is already hard (door open, empty hand, env start pose: 0.10), and the
drop is a one-action skill that deserves its own node. A skill that is too hard for a primitive is
decomposed; that is what the hierarchy is for.

**One re-specification only.** If the nodes below do not stabilise within the budgets below, that is
the finding, H-7a stops, and it is reported as is. No third attempt.

**The failed attempt stays on the record.** Rename `runs/v2/pickup_box_s{42,43,44}.json` to
`pickup_box_attempt1_s*.json` and `h73_pickup_box_s*.log` likewise; keep
`pickup_box_diagnosis.json`. The summary and the amortisation table state that this skill took two
specifications and list the first attempt's cost (3 × 600,064 env steps) under caveat 4.

## 4. Node specifications

```
{0,9} drop_held                                primitive, m=9
  goal        carrying_nothing()
  initiation  carrying_anything()              # new predicate, spec "carrying:any"
  timeout     30
  env_ids     [MiniGrid-UnlockPickup-v0]
  hook        compose(open_door(), give_key(), randomize_agent_pose(anchor=None))
  budget      100,000 env steps, eval_every 10,000

{0,4} pickup_box                               primitive, m=4   (new spec, new spec_hash)
  goal        carrying("box")
  initiation  all_of(door_open(), carrying_nothing())
  timeout     120
  env_ids     [MiniGrid-UnlockPickup-v0]
  hook        compose(open_door(), key_off_door(), randomize_agent_pose(anchor=None))
  budget      1,000,000 env steps, eval_every 20,000

{2,2} unlock_pickup                            composite, n=2, m=2
  children      ({1,1} unlock_door, {0,9} drop_held, {0,4} pickup_box)
  goal          carrying("box")
  initiation    door_locked()
  env           MiniGrid-UnlockPickup-v0, no reset hook
  timeout       288
  max_decisions 8
  n_steps 64, eval_every 512, budget 40,960 decisions      # as the plan

{1,B} flat_unlockpickup_options               baseline only
  children      (get_key, open_door, drop_held, pickup_box)
  goal carrying("box"), timeout 288, max_decisions 12, same training settings
```

Two new library functions, each with a spec string:

- `carrying_anything()` in `src/skills/predicates.py`, spec `"carrying:any"`.
- `key_off_door()` in `src/envs/subgoal_env.py`, spec `"key_off_door"`: raise `UnsuitableLayout` when
  a key lies on a cell orthogonally adjacent to the door. With the door open and the hand empty, a
  key on the door's approach cell can only be passed by picking it up, which fills the hand again.
  The pre-flight counted 13 such layouts in 500. At the real handoff it cannot happen, because the
  agent is standing on that cell when it drops the key.

Notes on the choices:

- `pickup_box`'s pose is drawn over both rooms, as before. About half the starts are in the left
  room, which is where the parent hands over, and the right-room starts give the early reward. The
  gate is the real handoff, not the hook distribution.
- 1,000,000 is fixed now and is not extended. Attempt 1 learned only its easiest starts by 600k,
  with about a quarter of its experience on them; this spec puts half its experience there.
- Under the goal-only mask `drop_held` is unavailable while the hand is empty (its goal already
  holds) and `pickup_box` stays selectable with a full hand, where it will time out. The selector has
  to learn unlock → drop → pick up from that; the strict mask gives it for free. Both are reported.
