## What this does and does not establish

- It removes the explanation H-6 left open: with a final wrong pick the 0.78–0.80 goal-only scores
  of H-6 disappear (they measured recovery), and the learned score falls to the constants' level.
- It does not say why. H-6.9 measured that the selector's own network reads the colour supervised
  (0.940 from 1,000 examples), so the colour is decodable from the 147-dim observation; what fails
  here is learning it from the 2-decision reward within 100 updates.
- Not examined: more updates, other entropy settings, the selector's probabilities (scoring is
  argmax), and a physically irreversible variant of the task.

## Limits

score cannot be STABLE (ceiling 0.93 on the fresh seeds), so no run stopped early.
